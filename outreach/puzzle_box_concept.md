# Jigsaw Puzzle Mailer Concept

## The Metaphor

Every plant's production picture is missing a few pieces. Myarra finds
them.

## The Physical Package

- **Flat pack:** Rigid chipboard mailer envelope, plain or lightly
  branded, hand-addressed to the plant manager by name.
- **Contents:** A custom jigsaw puzzle, with three to four pieces
  removed before shipping. A printed insert card. The Myarra one-pager.
- **The puzzle:** A stock photo of a production floor, printed as a
  jigsaw by a consumer photo service (Walgreens, CVS, or an online
  service). 8x10 or 11x14.
- **The missing pieces:** Held back, not shipped. Let the puzzle be
  incomplete. That is the point.

## The Copy

**Envelope label or printed on the flat pack:**

> Do not assemble until the missing pieces arrive.

**Insert card — front:**

> **Your production picture is missing a few pieces.**

**Insert card — back:**

> Every plant has hidden capacity. It lives in the minutes your downtime
> log cannot explain. The parts of the picture you cannot see.
>
> We built a private, voice-first appliance that finds those missing
> pieces. It measures true OEE against operator performance, without
> cloud subscriptions or data leaving your building.
>
> **We will find your missing minutes.**
> Schedule an Operational Diagnostic.
> **artifexofabrika.com/#diagnostic**
> [Phone number]

## The Two Taglines

- **Box label:** Solve the puzzle of production performance.
- **Insert card:** Every plant is missing a few pieces. The picture is
  almost complete. Almost.

## Sourcing Notes

Stock images: Pexels, Unsplash, Burst, or StockCake (all free for
commercial use, no attribution required). Choose images with distinct,
contrasting zones (conveyor belts, machinery, floor markings) so the
puzzle is not uniform and the missing pieces are noticeable.

## Execution Checklist

1. Order flat packs (Amazon, Uline, or a local packaging supplier).
2. Order the puzzle prints from a consumer photo service. Choose a
   production floor image.
3. On arrival, open one puzzle per envelope and remove 3 to 4 pieces.
   Hold them back.
4. Print the insert cards on heavier stock, two sides.
5. Assemble: puzzle bag, insert card, one-pager, into the flat pack.
6. Hand-address the envelope to the plant manager by name.
7. Seal with the label or sticker.
