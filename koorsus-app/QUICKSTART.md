# Arkiv Quick Start

Welcome. This card tells you everything you need to get Arkiv working in
your office. Keep it with the appliance. If something goes wrong, this is
the first place to look.

## What is in the box

- The Arkiv appliance
- A power adapter
- An Ethernet cable
- This Quick Start card
- Your PIN card (kept separately, in your password manager)

## Before you begin

You will need:

- An electrical outlet
- An available port on your office network router or switch
- A computer, laptop, or tablet on the same office network

## Step 1: Connect the appliance

1. Plug the power adapter into the appliance and into a wall outlet.
2. Plug the Ethernet cable into the appliance and into your office network.
3. Wait two minutes while the appliance starts.

## Step 2: Open Arkiv

On a computer or tablet on your office network:

1. Open your web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox).
2. Type the address printed on the back of this card into the address bar.
3. Press Enter.

You should see the Arkiv sign-in page with a navy blue header.

If the page does not load, see "If something goes wrong" at the end.

## Step 3: Sign in

1. Enter the PIN from your PIN card.
2. Click Sign in.

If this is the first use, your office manager should sign in first, set a
strong 16-character PIN, and store it in the firm's password manager.

## Step 4: Add your first document

1. Click Ingest in the top navigation bar.
2. Click "Choose file" and select a document from your computer. Arkiv
   accepts .txt, .md, .pdf, .docx, and .odt files.
3. You may select several files at once. All of them will be tagged with
   the same matter.
4. Fill in the two fields:
   - Uploaded by: your name
   - Matter reference: the case number or client name
     (for example: "2026-0142 Henderson")
5. Click "Upload and ingest".

Arkiv will confirm how many files were ingested and how many chunks each
produced.

## Step 5: Ask your first question

1. Click Ask in the top navigation bar.
2. (Optional) Choose a matter from the Scope dropdown. For the first
   question, leave it on "All matters".
3. Type a question in plain English, for example: "What is the lease term
   for the Henderson matter?"
4. Click Ask.

Arkiv will answer from your documents and list the source documents it
used. If the answer is not in the documents, Arkiv will say so plainly.
That is by design. An honest refusal is better than a confident wrong
answer.

## What Arkiv does

- Answers questions from your firm's documents, with citations.
- Keeps your documents on the appliance, in your office.
- Never connects to the internet.
- Refuses to answer when the information is not in the documents.

## What Arkiv does not do

- It does not give legal advice.
- It does not replace your case management system, your billing system, or
  your legal research databases.
- It does not draft documents. It answers questions from documents you
  have provided.

## Your documents are private

- Your documents are encrypted on the appliance.
- The appliance is not connected to the internet. It has no way to send
  your data anywhere.
- Your backups are encrypted with a passphrase you set. The passphrase is
  stored in your password manager, not on the appliance.
- If the appliance is stolen, your documents cannot be read.

## Backups

Arkiv backs up your documents automatically every night at 3:00 AM. The
backup goes to the USB drive provided and is encrypted with your office
backup passphrase.

The safe ritual, once a week:

1. Friday, before leaving: insert the backup USB drive into the appliance.
2. Monday morning: remove the USB drive and return it to your safe.

Three nightly backups run over the weekend while the drive is inserted.
The drive is not in the appliance during the work week.

The backup passphrase is on your PIN card. Store it in the firm's password
manager, and make sure at least two people in the firm have access to it.

If you lose the backup passphrase, your backups are unrecoverable.
Artifexo does not hold a copy.

## If something goes wrong

The appliance has no lights.
  Check that the power adapter is fully inserted at both ends and that
  the wall outlet works.

The browser cannot reach Arkiv.
  Check that the appliance is connected to the office network and that
  your computer is on the same network. If you have moved the appliance,
  check the Ethernet cable at both ends.

The PIN does not work.
  Contact your office manager, who holds the master record.

A document will not ingest.
  Check that it is one of the supported formats: .txt, .md, .pdf, .docx,
  or .odt. Some PDFs are image-only scans and cannot be read. Contact
  Artifexo if you need this.

The answer seems wrong.
  Choose a specific matter from the Scope dropdown and try again. If the
  answer is still wrong, contact Artifexo with the question and the
  documents you expected it to use.

The appliance is damaged or will not start.
  Contact Artifexo. We will ship you a replacement. Your backup USB has
  everything you need to restore your documents on the new appliance.

## First day checklist

- [ ] Appliance is connected to power and network
- [ ] Office manager has signed in and set a strong 16-character PIN
- [ ] PIN and backup passphrase are stored in the firm's password manager
- [ ] At least two documents have been ingested under a matter
- [ ] One test question answered and its sources checked
- [ ] The backup USB drive is in the office safe

## Warranty

Your Arkiv appliance is warranted for one year from the date of delivery.

If the appliance fails, contact us before 12:00 noon Eastern and we will
arrange a replacement shipped the same day where possible, or the next
business day otherwise.

To restore on a replacement appliance: insert your backup USB, run one
command (we will send exact instructions), and you are back to work.
