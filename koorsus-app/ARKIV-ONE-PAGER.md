# Arkiv

## RAG for less than a laptop.

Your firm's documents, reachable in plain English. On an appliance in your office. Never on anyone else's server.

---

### The problem

Your documents are the substance of your practice. Contracts, pleadings, discovery, correspondence, prior work product. None of them are reachable by search. The lawyer remembers the case, not the paragraph. The paralegal knows which file has the clause, until they leave.

The general-purpose AI tools solve this by sending your documents to someone else's server. For a firm whose privilege obligations follow every page, that is not a solution.

### What Arkiv is

A small appliance that sits in your office, connects to your network, and answers questions from your own documents. It is not a cloud service. It is not a subscription. It is a box you own.

You ingest your documents by matter. You ask questions in plain English. Arkiv answers from those documents, cites the exact source of every claim, and refuses when the answer is not there.

### What it does

- Answers questions from your firm's documents, with the source passage quoted.
- Organises documents by matter. A firm with a hundred active cases gets the right answer, not the closest one.
- Ingests text, Markdown, PDF, Word, and OpenDocument files. Multiple at once.
- Runs entirely on the appliance. No internet connection. No telemetry.

### What it does not do

- It does not give legal advice. It answers from documents and cites them. You draw the conclusions.
- It does not replace your case management system, billing system, or legal research databases.
- It does not connect to the internet. Ever. By design.
- It does not answer when the answer is not in the documents. **The refusal is the feature.** A confident wrong answer about a filing deadline or a contract term is a liability. An honest "I do not find that" is not.

### Why it can be trusted

Every answer cites the source document and the passage it drew from. You read the source and the answer side by side. Checking the work takes ten seconds.

When the documents do not contain the answer, Arkiv refuses. That is not a limitation; it is the reason you can trust the answers it does give. A system that guesses is worse than no system at all.

### Your documents stay in your office

The appliance has an Ethernet cable to your office network and nothing else. There is no outbound connection, no telemetry, no update check, no remote support tunnel. Your documents never leave your building.

The disk is encrypted. The backups are encrypted with a passphrase your firm chooses and stores in its password manager. Artifexo does not hold your data and cannot read it.

If the appliance is stolen, your documents cannot be read.

### If the appliance fails

Call us before noon Eastern. We ship a replacement the same day, or the next business day.

Your replacement arrives with Arkiv pre-installed. Insert your backup USB drive, type your passphrase, and your documents are back. Same day, no cloud, no download, no waiting for us to restore your data.

Every backup is checksummed when written and verified weekly by an automatic restore. If a backup has failed, you will know before you need it.
