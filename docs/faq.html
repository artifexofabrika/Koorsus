Questions

Arkiv, answered the way we would answer them across a table.

Does it need the internet?

No. It runs on your office network, on one cable to your switch or router, and it makes no outbound connections of its own. Nothing about your documents leaves the building.

What does it cost to run?

About twenty-five watts at most, so a few dollars of electricity a month. There is no other running cost, because there is no subscription and no cloud account.

Can it read scanned documents?

Only if the file already contains text. A PDF made from Word, or a filing downloaded from a court system, usually does. Paper put through a copier usually does not until it has been through OCR. Arkiv tells you when a document yields nothing rather than guessing at it.

Where do my documents live?

On the appliance, in your office. Nothing is copied to us, and there is no cloud account to lose access to.

Do you have access to my files?

No. We never connect to the appliance and we do not hold a copy of anything on it. The backup is encrypted with a passphrase generated at installation, held on the appliance so the nightly job can run unattended, and kept by your firm in its password manager. We do not hold one.

Is there a subscription?

No. One price, and the device is yours. Four hours of support by video call are included, as is email support for the first thirty days, and a support plan afterwards is a hundred dollars an hour.

What happens if it fails?

Call us before noon Eastern. We ship a replacement the same day, or the next business day. Copy the archive from your backup stick onto it, and your documents, your accounts and your query log come back, including which matters you had closed. We do not touch your data.

Can staff use it on phones and tablets?

Yes, on the office network, at no extra cost. Each device trusts the appliance once, which we set up during installation, and after that it opens like a website.

Can I use it from home?

That is an upgrade, at five hundred dollars. It needs a VPN into your network, and it means opening a path into that network, which is a decision for whoever runs it rather than something we switch on quietly.

Can we change the software?

Yes. You receive the source and you can modify it for your own use, or hire anyone you like to do so. The licence does not let you resell it as a competing product.

Does it give legal advice?

No. It answers from your documents and quotes the passage. You draw the conclusions.

How many people can use it at once?

Two at once on the standard appliance. A third waits a few seconds rather than being refused, and ingesting a large document is the one thing that makes others wait. Each person has their own account, and every question is recorded with who asked it, which matter it concerned, and whether it refused to answer.

What if we outgrow it?

Larger appliances are available to order, and the software is the same, so nothing is learned twice and nothing is thrown away. Storage starts at half a terabyte, with a terabyte for a hundred dollars more and two for two hundred.

Still a question.

Write to us and we will answer it plainly, whether or not you buy anything.
