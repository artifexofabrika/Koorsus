Questions

Arkiv, answered the way we would answer them across a table.

Does it need the internet?

No. It runs on your office network and answers questions with no internet connection at all. One cable to your switch or router is the whole requirement.

What does it cost to run?

About twenty-five watts at most, so a few dollars of electricity a month. There is no other running cost, because there is no subscription and no cloud account.

Can it read scanned documents?

Only if the scan already contains text, which most office scanners produce. A photograph of a page, or a scan with no text layer, cannot be read. Arkiv tells you when a document yields nothing rather than guessing at it.

Where do my documents live?

On the appliance, in your office. Nothing is copied to us, and there is no cloud account to lose access to.

Do you have access to my files?

No. We never connect to the appliance and we do not hold a copy of anything on it. The backup USB is yours, encrypted with a passphrase your firm chooses.

Is there a subscription?

No. One price, and the device is yours. Optional support is a hundred dollars an hour if you ever want it.

What happens if it fails?

Call us before noon Eastern. We ship a replacement the same day, or the next business day. Your backup USB and passphrase restore everything on the new appliance, without us touching your data.

Can staff use it on phones and tablets?

Yes, on the office network, at no extra cost. Anyone on your network can open it on a phone or tablet and use it like a website.

Can I use it from home?

That is an upgrade, at five hundred dollars. It needs a VPN into your network, and it means opening a path into that network, which is a decision for whoever runs it rather than something we switch on quietly.

Can we change the software?

Yes. You receive the source and you can modify it, or hire anyone you like to do so. It is your device and nobody is holding the code back.

Does it give legal advice?

No. It answers from your documents and cites the passage. You draw the conclusions.

How many people can use it at once?

Several, each with their own account. Every question is recorded with who asked it, which matter it concerned, and whether it refused to answer.

What if we outgrow it?

Larger modules are available to order. The software is the same, so nothing is learned twice and nothing is thrown away.

Still a question.

Write to us and we will answer it plainly, whether or not you buy anything.
