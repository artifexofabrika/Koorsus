The object.

We believe your private AI should be as elegant as the rest of your home. No plastic servers in the closet. No blinking lights in the hallway.

Jeeves is an appliance. It sits on your desk or your bookshelf. It is milled aluminum, wrapped in leather, accented with brass. It draws less power than a reading lamp. It is designed to be seen.

What it will do.

A private assistant for the things you do not want in the cloud.

The Confidant A conversational AI that runs locally. No token limits, no monthly fees, no cloud provider reading your chats. It learns your preferences and rewrites its own personality to match how you want to be spoken to.
The Knowledge Lake Save snippets, notes, and documents. Retrieve them semantically. Search your own archive instead of the web.
The Trading Desk Paper trading and crypto simulation with strict risk limits. An exchange-agnostic broker interface for simulated strategies. No cloud, no API keys leaking to third parties.
The Wellness Tracker Structured logging of meals, medication, mood, and health metrics. Natural-language queries over your own health history.

Privacy, by design.

The general-purpose AI tools solve problems by sending your data to someone else's server. For your health, your finances, and your private correspondence, that is not a solution.

Jeeves runs entirely on your local network. No API calls, no telemetry, no account. It works air-gapped. The model, the data, and the code are yours.

Under the hood.

Jeeves runs entirely on an NVIDIA Jetson. The base unit uses the Orin NX; the performance tier uses the AGX Thor. It runs a quantized LLM locally via llama.cpp.

For developers: the open-source core is available on GitHub under the MIT license.
