The object.

Your private AI should be as considered as the rest of your home. No plastic server in a closet. No blinking lights in the hall.

Jeeves is an appliance. It sits on a desk or a bookshelf. Leather, brass, and milled aluminum, designed to be seen and to draw less power than a desktop computer.

The Gentleman's Valet. Leather, brass plate, no visible hardware.

What it will do.

A private assistant for the parts of your life you do not want in the cloud.

The Confidant A conversational AI that runs locally. No token limits, no monthly fee, no provider reading your conversations. It will learn your preferences and match the way you want to be spoken to.
The Knowledge Lake Save notes, snippets, and documents. Retrieve them by meaning rather than by filename. Search your own archive instead of the web.
The Trading Desk Paper trading and portfolio simulation with strict risk caps. Strategies are tested against simulated positions. No brokerage credentials required, and nothing can be executed for real.
The Wellness Ledger Structured logging of meals, sleep, medication, and mood. Ask questions in plain English of your own health history.

Privacy, by design.

General-purpose AI tools work by sending your data to someone else's server. For your health, your finances, and your private correspondence, that is not a solution.

Jeeves runs entirely on your own network. No accounts, no telemetry, no outbound calls to a model provider. The model, the data, and the software are yours.

Under the hood.

Base units run on an NVIDIA Jetson Orin NX with a quantized language model served locally. The performance tier steps up to the AGX Thor for larger models and a bigger knowledge lake.

The software core is developed in the open at github.com/artifexofabrika/Jeeves---public.
