The problem.

Your documents are the substance of your practice. Contracts, pleadings, discovery, correspondence, prior work product. Almost none of it is reachable by search. The lawyer remembers the case, not the paragraph. The paralegal knows which file has the clause, until they leave.

General-purpose AI tools solve this by sending your documents to someone else's server. For a firm whose privilege obligations follow every page, that is not a solution.

What Arkiv does.

Your documents, answered with the source cited. Refused when the answer is not there.

What it does

  • Answers questions from your own documents in plain English.
  • Organises documents by matter. A firm with a hundred active cases gets the right answer, not the closest one.
  • Quotes the passage behind every answer, so you can check the work in ten seconds.
  • Refuses when the answer is not in the documents. The refusal is the feature.
  • Gives every member of staff their own account and PIN, and records every question against the name of whoever asked it.
  • Runs on the appliance in your office, on your office network. It makes no outbound connections of its own.
  • Backs itself up every night into an encrypted archive, opens the newest one every week to prove it can still be read, and copies to a USB stick when you ask it to.

*PDF support requires a text layer. Scanned paper that has not been processed by Optical Character Recognition (OCR) cannot be read.

What it does not do

  • It does not give legal advice. It answers from documents and cites them. You draw the conclusions.
  • It does not replace your case management system, billing system, or legal research databases.
  • It does not answer every question. When the documents do not contain the answer, it says so.

Point-and-shoot ingestion.

No data engineers. No manual chunking. No ETL scripts.

Most AI projects die in data preparation. They need custom scripts, formatting rules, and someone technical to get the documents in. Arkiv does not.

The paralegal selects the files, and the appliance reads the matter from the filename. Nothing is typed in per file and there is no spreadsheet to maintain. Documents named like matter_12_petition.txt are filed under Matter 12 automatically; anything without a matter number is filed wherever you say once, for the whole batch.

Then the appliance does the work: it extracts the text, splits it into searchable sections, tags every piece with the uploader and the matter, and indexes it. A hundred documents across thirty matters takes minutes rather than an afternoon, and afterwards you can see every document listed under its matter, with a delete beside it if something went in by mistake.

Pick the files. The matter comes from the filename. That is the whole procedure.

Why it can be trusted.

Every answer carries the source passage, not just the file name. You read the source and the answer side by side. Checking the work takes ten seconds.

When the documents do not contain the answer, Arkiv refuses. Retrieval happens before the model runs; if there is nothing relevant, the system refuses without calling the language model at all. If there is something, the prompt instructs it to say plainly when the answer is not there. The refusal is a code path, not a hope. A system that guesses is worse than no system at all.

Your documents stay in your office.

The appliance has an Ethernet cable to your office network and nothing else. No outbound connection, no telemetry, no update check, no remote support tunnel. Your documents never leave your building.

Reaching it from outside the office is a separate decision, and one worth saying plainly. It means opening a path into your firm's network through a VPN, which is a choice for whoever runs that network rather than something we should switch on quietly. We install it as an upgrade at $500.

Backups are encrypted with a passphrase generated at installation. It is held on the appliance so the nightly job can run unattended, and your firm keeps a copy in its password manager. Artifexo does not hold one and cannot read your documents.

If the appliance fails, call us before noon Eastern. We ship a replacement the same day, or the next business day. Copy the archive from your backup stick onto it and your documents, your accounts and your query log come back, including which matters were closed. No cloud, no download, no waiting for us to restore your data.
