Arkiv

RAG for less than a laptop.

Your firm's documents, reachable in plain English. On an appliance in your office. Never on anyone else's server.

The problem.

Your documents are the substance of your practice. Contracts, pleadings, discovery, correspondence, prior work product. Almost none of it is reachable by search. The lawyer remembers the case, not the paragraph. The paralegal knows which file has the clause, until they leave.

General-purpose AI tools solve this by sending your documents to someone else's server. For a firm whose privilege obligations follow every page, that is not a solution.

What Arkiv does.

Your documents, answered with the source cited. Refused when the answer is not there.

What it does

  • Answers questions from your own documents in plain English.
  • Organises documents by matter. A firm with a hundred active cases gets the right answer, not the closest one.
  • Cites the exact source passage behind every claim.
  • Refuses when the answer is not in the documents. The refusal is the feature.
  • Runs entirely on the appliance. No internet, no telemetry.
  • Backs itself up nightly, encrypted, to a USB you can keep in your safe or in your cloud.

What it does not do

  • It does not give legal advice. It answers from documents and cites them. You draw the conclusions.
  • It does not replace your case management system, billing system, or legal research databases.
  • It does not connect to the internet. Ever. By design.
  • It does not read scanned PDFs with no text layer. Most documents generated from Word or a court filing system have a text layer; scanned paper does not.
  • It does not answer every question. When the documents do not contain the answer, it says so.

Why it can be trusted.

Every answer carries the source passage, not just the file name. You read the source and the answer side by side. Checking the work takes ten seconds.

When the documents do not contain the answer, Arkiv refuses. Retrieval happens before the model runs; if there is nothing relevant, the system refuses without calling the language model at all. If there is something, the prompt instructs it to say plainly when the answer is not there. The refusal is a code path, not a hope. A system that guesses is worse than no system at all.

Every refusal is logged alongside every answer. A firm can audit what the system declined to answer and whether the refusal was correct.

Your documents stay in your office.

The appliance has an Ethernet cable to your office network and nothing else. No outbound connection, no telemetry, no update check, no remote support tunnel. Your documents never leave your building.

The disk is encrypted. The backups are encrypted with a passphrase your firm chooses and stores in its password manager. Artifexo does not hold your data and cannot read it. If the appliance is stolen, your documents cannot be read.

If the appliance fails, call us before noon Eastern. We ship a replacement the same day, or the next business day. Your backup USB and passphrase restore everything on the new appliance. No cloud, no download, no waiting for us to restore your data.
