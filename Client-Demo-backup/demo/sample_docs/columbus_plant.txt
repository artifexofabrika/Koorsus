Columbus Plant — Operational Summary

The Columbus facility reduced changeover time on Line 3 from 45 minutes to
12 minutes by applying Single-Minute Exchange of Die (SMED) principles over
a six-week kaizen event in March. The primary gains came from converting
internal setup tasks to external tasks, standardizing tool carts, and
introducing a two-person parallel workflow during the die change.

Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE) on Line 3 improved from 61% to 78%
following the changeover project. Availability rose from 84% to 91%, and
performance improved from 79% to 88%. Quality remained steady at 97%.

The plant's safety record in the most recent quarter: zero lost-time
injuries, two recordable incidents, both minor lacerations. The safety
committee meets every Tuesday at 7:00 AM in the conference room adjacent to
the shipping dock.

Inventory turns improved from 6.2 to 8.4 annually after the kanban
implementation in the parts warehouse. The warehouse now maintains three
days of raw material buffer, down from twelve. The kanban cards are
reviewed every Monday morning by the materials supervisor.
